Refund & Cancellation Policy

Home / Refund Policy

Effective Date: 1st January 2024 | Last Updated: November 2024

At Vibe Seekers, we stand behind the premium craftsmanship of our streetwear. If you are not completely satisfied with your order, we offer a straightforward, hassle-free 7-Day Return and Exchange Policy.

1. Order Cancellation Policy

  • Before Dispatch: You can cancel your order at any time before it has been marked as Packed or Shipped. Contact our support via WhatsApp or email with your Order ID. For prepaid orders cancelled prior to dispatch, a 100% full refund is issued instantly.
  • After Dispatch: Once handed over to our courier partner and an AWB has been generated, orders cannot be cancelled mid-transit. You may instead receive the parcel and initiate a standard 7-day return.

2. 7-Day Return & Exchange Window

You can request a return or size exchange within 7 days from the date of confirmed delivery.

✓ Eligible Conditions for Return:

  • Garments must be entirely unworn, unwashed, unaltered, and free of perfumes, stains, or deodorant marks.
  • All original brand tags, labels, and hygiene strips must remain attached in their original condition.
  • The item must be returned inside its original packaging/polybag.

✕ Non-Returnable Items:

  • Clearance / Flash Sale items marked explicitly as "Final Sale".
  • Undergarments, socks, or accessories due to hygiene protocols.
  • Items returned without original tags, damaged, or laundered.

3. Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Items

In the rare event that you receive a defective garment, incorrect size, or print error, please report it within 48 hours of delivery by sending photos of the item and courier packaging to {{ site_settings.get('store_email', 'hello@vibeseekers.in') }} or WhatsApp {{ site_settings.get('store_phone', '+91 98604 98749') }}. We will arrange a free reverse pickup and immediate priority replacement at zero additional cost to you.

4. Refund Mode & Timeline

Once your returned parcel reaches our fulfillment warehouse and passes quality inspection (usually within 24–48 hours of receipt):

  • Prepaid Orders (Razorpay): Refunds are processed directly back to the original source payment method (UPI, credit/debit card, net banking). The funds reflect in your account within 5 to 7 business days as per banking network standards.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: Refunds are credited via direct NEFT / IMPS bank transfer or secure UPI transfer. Our customer support will contact you to collect your beneficiary account details.

5. How to Initiate a Return or Exchange

1
Email us at {{ site_settings.get('store_email', 'hello@vibeseekers.in') }} with subject line "Return Request - [Your Order ID]" or WhatsApp {{ site_settings.get('store_phone', '+91 98604 98749') }}.
2
State the reason for return/exchange and mention whether you want a different size or a refund.
3

4
Hand over the packaged garment with tags to the pickup executive. Your refund or replacement is dispatched immediately upon warehouse arrival.
