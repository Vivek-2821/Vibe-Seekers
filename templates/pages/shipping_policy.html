FREE Shipping Pan-India on Orders Above ₹999

All orders of subtotal ₹999 or more automatically qualify for 100% Free Standard Shipping across India. No coupon code needed!

1. Shipping Rates & Charges

  • Orders Above ₹999: FREE Standard Delivery across all serviceable Indian pincodes.
  • Orders Below ₹999: A nominal flat shipping fee of ₹99 applies to cover insured logistics.
  • Shipping charges are calculated and displayed transparently in your Cart and on the Checkout summary before payment.

2. Order Processing & Dispatch

Every order is carefully inspected, folded, and custom-packed at our fulfillment warehouse:

  • Processing Time: 24 to 48 business hours from the time payment is verified (or COD order placed).
  • Orders placed on Sundays or national public holidays will be dispatched on the next working business day.
  • You will receive an automated dispatch email with your tracking details the moment your package is handed over to the courier.

3. Estimated Delivery Times

We partner with premium express couriers to deliver your streetwear as swiftly as possible:

  • Metro Cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata): 2 to 4 business days post-dispatch.
  • Rest of India (Tier 2 & 3 Cities, Regional Towns): 4 to 7 business days post-dispatch.
  • North-East, J&K, Island Territories: 6 to 9 business days depending on air connectivity.

Note: Occasional transit delays may occur during extreme weather conditions, election periods, or peak festive sale rushes. We appreciate your patience.

4. Order Tracking

As soon as your package ships, you will receive an email containing your AWB / Tracking Number and direct tracking URL. You can also live-track your parcel at any time through our Track Order Page or directly in My Orders.

5. Courier Partners

Our shipments are fulfilled through India's premier logistics aggregators and courier networks, including Delhivery, Bluedart, DTDC, Shadowfax, Xpressbees, and Ekart.

6. Damaged / Tampered Parcels

If the outer courier polybag appears visibly tampered with, damaged, or unsealed upon delivery, please do not accept the package. Request the delivery agent to mark it as rejected due to outer box damage and contact us immediately at {{ site_settings.get('store_email', 'hello@vibeseekers.in') }} so we can dispatch an expedited replacement.
